# Ideas

A parking lot for future product directions for Ember. Append new ideas as dated subsections so we can revisit them later.

## 2026-05-03 — Co-tending dragons (shared goals & habits)

**Concept:** Let multiple people co-tend / co-own the same dragon, instead of dragons being strictly single-user companions.

**Shape of the idea:**
- A dragon can have more than one caretaker (partners, friends, family, teammates, accountability buddies).
- Co-tenders share goals and/or habits tied to that dragon — e.g. a couple sharing a "walk every day" habit, roommates sharing a "tidy the kitchen" goal, a study group sharing a "review flashcards" habit.
- Care actions (feeding, training, check-ins) from any co-tender contribute to the dragon's wellbeing, so the dragon's state reflects the *group's* collective follow-through, not just one person's.
- Co-care dynamics: visibility into who tended last, gentle nudges when one person has been carrying it, small shared rituals (e.g. both check in → bonus moment).

**Why it felt compelling:**
- Extends the dragon-as-companion metaphor into shared accountability — the dragon becomes a living shared object that embodies a relationship's habits, not just one person's discipline.
- Naturally social without being a "social network" — the surface area stays intimate (small groups, real relationships).
- Opens up use cases Ember doesn't currently serve: couples, families, small teams, study buddies, recovery partners.

**Open questions for later:**
- Ownership & permissions: who can edit goals, rename the dragon, remove a co-tender?
- What happens to the dragon if co-tenders drift apart or one leaves?
- How do individual streaks vs. shared streaks coexist?
- Privacy: what does each co-tender see about the others' contributions?

**Status:** Parked — not building now. Revisit once single-user Ember has clearer product-market fit.
